## English

**Title:** How an MoU or MoA Gets Signed at an Indonesian University

**Excerpt:** The seven steps I follow at PETRA to take a partnership agreement from final text to a signed, published and active MoU or MoA, and what keeps it moving.

### Body

A signing ceremony lasts perhaps an hour. The photo of two leaders holding a folder is what both institutions publish, and it is what most people remember. But the ceremony is the visible end of a long process, and if that process is weak the agreement either stalls before the ceremony or sits in a drawer after it.

At Petra Christian University (PETRA) I review more than 25 partnership agreements a month and bring many of them to signature. This post sets out how an agreement moves from final text to an active partnership, and the checks I use to keep it moving. The detail is PETRA's, but the shape will be familiar to most Indonesian universities.

## MoU or MoA: what is actually being signed

The two terms are often used loosely, so it is worth being precise.

- **An MoU (Memorandum of Understanding, or *nota kesepahaman*)** is a framework. It records that two institutions intend to work together and names the broad areas: student exchange, research, staff development, curriculum.
- **An MoA (Memorandum of Agreement, or *perjanjian kerja sama*)** is more specific. It usually sits under an MoU, or stands alone with a narrower scope, and sets out concrete activities, the units responsible, and what each side commits.

In practice, an MoU opens the door and an MoA says who walks through it and when. Activities under either are then documented with an Implementation Arrangement (IA) and reported with an Implementation Report (IR). ## Before the signing: the agreement lifecycle

Every agreement at PETRA passes through eight stages: draft, faculty review, approval, partner review, signature, active, renewal and archive. The first four happen before anyone books a room.

The proposing faculty or unit drafts the scope and commitments and checks the fit with its own plans. Approval then runs in a fixed order through the university hierarchy. In SIM Kerjasama, the system I designed for agreements, that order is enforced in software in three tiers: the head of the International Office and the head of the Rector's Secretariat; then deans and heads of programmes and units; then the Vice Rectors and the Rector in parallel. An approver can approve, ask for a revision, put the document on hold, or reject it. Finally the partner reviews the final text.

You can see the full lifecycle on the [MoU and MoA coordination page](/mou).

## Seven steps from final text to activation

Once both sides have agreed the text and every approval is in, the signing process itself has seven steps.

### 1. Final text

Lock the agreed text with the partner, in both languages where needed. "Final" means no further edits: any change after this point restarts the approval chain, so I confirm in writing that both sides accept the version going to signature.

### 2. Approval

Check that the approval trail is complete and in order: proposing unit, legal review, leadership. In SIM Kerjasama the document reaches the status *Disetujui* (approved) only when every tier has signed off.

### 3. Logistics

Set the date, the signatories, the venue, the run of show and the copies to be signed. Signatory names and titles are checked against the text before the day, not on it. [CONFIRM: number of original copies normally prepared, and whether stamp duty (*meterai*) is applied]

### 4. Ceremony

Host the delegation, run the programme and guide each signatory through each copy. At this point the document is *Siap TTD* (ready to sign) in the system.

### 5. Signed

Check the originals, exchange them and archive them. Scans are filed the same day.

### 6. Publication

Share photos and a short news item with the partner and with PETRA's communications team, so both institutions tell the same story.

### 7. Activation

Upload the signed file to SIM Kerjasama, which sets the agreement to *Aktif*, and hand it to the units that will run the activities. This is the step most often skipped, and the one that decides whether anything happens next.

## Where agreements stall, and what helps

Before SIM Kerjasama, proposals travelled by email and paper. Nobody could say where a document was or who was holding it, and a draft could sit with one office for weeks without anyone noticing. Three habits, now built into the system, made the biggest difference.

**Give every approval a clock.** Each approver's time is counted in business days from when their tier opens, excluding weekends, holidays and time on hold. It turns yellow at two days and red at four. Nobody is named and shamed; the delay simply becomes visible.

**Offer more than yes or no.** When the only alternative to approving is rejecting, approvers sit on documents they are unsure about. A "revise" option, with a note, keeps the document moving.

**Record what was signed elsewhere.** Some agreements are signed during a visit abroad or at an event. The office can record them directly, so the dataset is complete and those agreements can still be activated and renewed.

## A checklist for the week of a signing

- Final text confirmed in writing by both sides
- Approval trail complete and in order
- Signatory names, titles and order of signing checked against the text
- Copies printed, checked and in signing order
- Run of show shared with the partner, protocol and media
- Photographer briefed on the one photo both institutions need
- Same day: originals archived, scans filed
- Same week: agreement active in SIM Kerjasama, handed to the implementing units, news shared

## After the ink

An agreement is only worth what is carried out under it. That is why the last step is activation, not the ceremony, and why renewal starts six months before the end date with evaluations from both the faculty and the partner. If you want to see how the record is kept from draft to archive, the [SIM Kerjasama case study](/sim-kerjasama) walks through the system and its business process.

## Perbedaan MoU dan MoA

Dua istilah ini sering dipakai bergantian, padahal fungsinya berbeda.

- **MoU (*Memorandum of Understanding*, nota kesepahaman)** adalah kerangka. Isinya pernyataan bahwa dua institusi berniat bekerja sama, beserta bidang-bidangnya secara umum: pertukaran mahasiswa, penelitian, pengembangan dosen, kurikulum.
- **MoA (*Memorandum of Agreement*, perjanjian kerja sama)** lebih rinci. Biasanya berada di bawah MoU, atau berdiri sendiri dengan cakupan yang lebih sempit, dan memuat kegiatan konkret, unit penanggung jawab, serta komitmen masing-masing pihak.

Sederhananya, MoU membuka pintu, sedangkan MoA menentukan siapa yang masuk dan kapan. Kegiatan di bawah keduanya kemudian didokumentasikan dengan *Implementation Arrangement* (IA) dan dilaporkan dengan *Implementation Report* (IR). ## Sebelum tanda tangan: siklus hidup naskah

Setiap naskah di PETRA melewati delapan tahap: draf, telaah fakultas, persetujuan, telaah mitra, penandatanganan, aktif, perpanjangan, dan arsip. Empat tahap pertama terjadi jauh sebelum ruang rapat dipesan.

Fakultas atau unit pengusul menyusun ruang lingkup dan komitmen, lalu memastikan kerja sama itu sejalan dengan rencananya. Setelah itu persetujuan berjalan berjenjang. Di SIM Kerjasama, sistem yang saya rancang untuk naskah kerja sama, urutan ini dijaga oleh perangkat lunak dalam tiga tingkat: Kepala Kantor Internasional dan Kepala Sekretariat Rektor; lalu dekan serta kepala program studi, program, dan unit; lalu para Wakil Rektor dan Rektor secara paralel. Setiap pemberi persetujuan bisa menyetujui, meminta revisi, menunda, atau menolak. Terakhir, mitra menelaah naskah final.

Siklus lengkapnya bisa dilihat di [halaman koordinasi MoU dan MoA](/mou).

## Tujuh langkah dari naskah final sampai aktif

Ketika naskah sudah disepakati dan semua persetujuan lengkap, proses penandatanganan sendiri terdiri atas tujuh langkah.

### 1. Naskah final

Kunci naskah yang sudah disepakati bersama mitra, dalam dua bahasa bila perlu. "Final" berarti tidak ada lagi perubahan. Perubahan sekecil apa pun setelah titik ini berarti persetujuan harus diulang, jadi saya selalu meminta konfirmasi tertulis dari kedua pihak atas versi yang akan ditandatangani.

### 2. Persetujuan

Pastikan jejak persetujuan lengkap dan urut: unit pengusul, telaah hukum, pimpinan. Di SIM Kerjasama, status naskah baru menjadi *Disetujui* setelah semua tingkat memberi persetujuan.

### 3. Persiapan teknis

Tetapkan tanggal, penanda tangan, tempat, susunan acara, dan rangkap naskah yang akan ditandatangani. Nama dan jabatan penanda tangan dicocokkan dengan naskah sebelum hari H, bukan pada hari H. [CONFIRM: jumlah rangkap asli yang biasa disiapkan, dan apakah memakai meterai]

### 4. Seremoni

Sambut delegasi, jalankan acara, dan dampingi setiap penanda tangan di setiap rangkap. Pada tahap ini status naskah di sistem adalah *Siap TTD*.

### 5. Ditandatangani

Periksa naskah asli, pertukarkan, lalu arsipkan. Hasil pindaian disimpan pada hari yang sama.

### 6. Publikasi

Kirim foto dan berita singkat kepada mitra dan tim humas PETRA, supaya kedua institusi menyampaikan cerita yang sama.

### 7. Aktivasi

Unggah naskah bertanda tangan ke SIM Kerjasama sehingga statusnya menjadi *Aktif*, lalu serahkan kepada unit yang akan menjalankan kegiatannya. Langkah inilah yang paling sering terlewat, padahal langkah ini yang menentukan apakah kerja sama berlanjut menjadi kegiatan.

## Di mana naskah biasanya macet

Sebelum ada SIM Kerjasama, usulan kerja sama berpindah lewat surel dan kertas. Tidak ada yang tahu naskah sedang di meja siapa, dan sebuah draf bisa tertahan berminggu-minggu di satu kantor tanpa disadari. Tiga kebiasaan berikut, yang kini tertanam di sistem, paling banyak membantu.

**Beri setiap persetujuan batas waktu.** Waktu setiap pemberi persetujuan dihitung dalam hari kerja sejak gilirannya dibuka, tanpa akhir pekan, hari libur, dan masa tunda. Indikatornya kuning di hari kedua dan merah di hari keempat. Tujuannya bukan menyalahkan orang, tetapi membuat keterlambatan terlihat.

**Sediakan pilihan selain setuju atau tolak.** Kalau satu-satunya alternatif adalah menolak, pemberi persetujuan cenderung menahan naskah yang masih meragukan. Opsi "revisi" dengan catatan membuat naskah tetap bergerak.

**Catat naskah yang ditandatangani di luar sistem.** Ada naskah yang ditandatangani saat kunjungan ke luar negeri atau di sebuah acara. Kantor bisa mencatatnya langsung, sehingga data tetap lengkap dan naskah itu tetap bisa diaktifkan dan diperpanjang.

## Daftar periksa minggu penandatanganan

- Naskah final dikonfirmasi tertulis oleh kedua pihak
- Jejak persetujuan lengkap dan urut
- Nama, jabatan, dan urutan penanda tangan cocok dengan naskah
- Rangkap naskah dicetak, diperiksa, dan disusun sesuai urutan tanda tangan
- Susunan acara dibagikan kepada mitra, protokol, dan media
- Fotografer tahu satu foto yang dibutuhkan kedua institusi
- Hari yang sama: naskah asli diarsipkan, pindaian disimpan
- Minggu yang sama: status *Aktif* di SIM Kerjasama, naskah diserahkan ke unit pelaksana, berita dipublikasikan
